My conclusion

I wouldn't immediately replace your two shortlisted options.

For your exact requirement of approximately:

2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM / 150 GB NVMe

xneelo still looks like the strongest option I've found, because its decoupled storage model is almost tailor-made for your situation.

However, I think Absolute Hosting is worth one enquiry before you make the final decision. Their pricing is aggressive enough that they could potentially beat both xneelo and HostKing if they can build you a 2/4 VPS with ~150 GB NVMe at a sensible price. Their current infrastructure also advertises AMD EPYC, NVMe, root access, unlimited bandwidth and a South African Tier 3 data centre. 
client.absolutehosting.co.za

So my shortlist after today's check would be:

🥇 xneelo — best fit for the exact requirement
🥈 Absolute Hosting — investigate, because the pricing could be substantially better
🥉 HostKing — excellent fixed package, but forced into 4/8 because of storage
Domains.co.za — credible, but doesn't beat xneelo once storage is added

I would not choose Telasera for this particular production server despite the attractive R199 price, because the storage is only 60 GB.
